# Grand Finale — Judge Rubric (10 questions, each 1–5)

Each question is answered with one score from 1 to 5; comments are not scored.
**1** missing or generic · **2** stated but vague · **3** meets the question ·
**4** clear and well reasoned · **5** strong, backed by evidence. Max total: 50.

| # | The judge asks | What earns a high score |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What exact problem are you solving, and who faces it? | A specific problem and a named group of people |
| 2 | Why does this need a solution built for Pakistan? | Pakistani conditions visible in a **design choice** (Urdu or a regional language, low bandwidth, low cost, local rules) |
| 3 | Walk us through how a user goes from start to result. | **One** clear user, **one** clear flow from input to useful output; scope done fully |
| 4 | Show it working, live. | It runs end to end in front of the judge |
| 5 | What exactly does the AI do here, and why is it needed? | The AI does real work; the team can **name the model or service** and why |
| 6 | Explain how one important part of your code works. | The team explains its own code and decisions with confidence |
| 7 | What is new about your approach compared with what already exists? | Knows the alternatives and what it does differently; the technology fits the problem |
| 8 | Who benefits first, and how many people could this reach? | A real first user group and a believable path to reach more |
| 9 | What would it take to keep this running after the hackathon? | A realistic view of cost, data, upkeep or partners |
| 10 | Clarity and honesty (judge's own assessment). | Clear presentation, **honest about built versus planned**, direct answers |

Judges mark each team on a portal by project ID (format `P` + five digits). Know ours.
